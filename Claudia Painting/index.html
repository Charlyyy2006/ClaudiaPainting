Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Is the estimate really free?
Yes. We come look at the space, talk about what you want, and give you a price — with no obligation to hire us. You'll know the cost before any work begins.
Do I need to move my furniture?
No. We move what needs moving, cover everything with drop cloths and plastic, and put it back where it belongs when we're finished.
Do you help pick the colors?
Happily. Tell us the feeling you're going for and we'll suggest options and finishes that work for that room and that light. The final call is always yours.
Who buys the paint?
Either way works. We can supply everything, or paint what you've already bought. Just tell us which when we put the estimate together.
How long does a job take?
A single room is usually a day or two. A full interior or exterior takes longer and depends on prep work and weather. We'll give you a realistic window with the estimate — and tell you if anything changes.
¿Hablan español?
Sí, por supuesto. Escríbanos por WhatsApp o llene el formulario en español — le respondemos en el mismo idioma.
